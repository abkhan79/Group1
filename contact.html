Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer delivery through our partnerships with major delivery services. Call us or order online for convenience.

Can I customize my order?

Absolutely! We pride ourselves on making orders your way. Let us know your preferences, and we'll customize your meal perfectly.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes, we have several vegetarian and vegan options available. Visit our menu page to see our full selection of plant-based meals.

What are your allergy policies?

We take allergies seriously. Please inform our staff of any food allergies when ordering, and we'll do our best to accommodate your needs safely.

Do you offer catering services?

Yes! We offer catering for small and large events. Contact us for special packages and bulk orders.

What are your payment methods?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment methods like Apple Pay and Google Pay.
